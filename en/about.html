About the club

We bring together those who love to explore the world

The Smart Travel Club grew out of a simple idea: travel should be more accessible. We give members direct access to wholesale prices, technology and people who are more fun to travel with.

2013

Since that year, we have been building the club and expanding its geography.

Our mission

To make sure that everyone who wants to can see the world. We remove the unnecessary links between the traveler and the service provider, and return the benefit to club members.

  • Direct access to prices without intermediary markups
  • Technology that saves time on planning
  • A community where you can always find advice and a travel companion
