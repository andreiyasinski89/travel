3. Who we may share data with

  • Email and communication providers: the form-forwarding service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives the form data and passes it to us by email; the Google email service (Gmail), where the messages arrive; messengers (WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.) if you write to us through them.
  • Site hosting: GitHub (GitHub Pages). It processes technical logs of requests to the site.
  • Services loaded by the site: Google Fonts (fonts) and Tailwind CDN (styles). When a page loads, your browser contacts their servers, and they receive your IP address.
  • Analytics services: Google Analytics 4 (Google) and Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft). The Google Analytics script loads when a page opens; until you consent, it sets no analytics cookies and receives only anonymised technical signals (Google Consent Mode). The Microsoft Clarity script loads only after you consent. When their scripts run, the services receive your IP address.
  • Storage of quiz requests and reviews: Google (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). The form data is sent to Google servers and written to a spreadsheet available to the site owner.
  • Country-by-IP services: api.country.is and ipwho.is. Your browser contacts them only if your browser language is not supported by the site: they receive your IP address and return a country code. The result is stored only in the browser tab’s memory.
  • Public authorities: only when required by law.

Providers that process data on our behalf are bound by contractual terms that comply with Art. 28 GDPR.

6. How long we keep data

  • Application and correspondence data: while we are handling your inquiry, and then for no longer than 12 months after the last contact, unless the law requires us to keep it longer or the data is needed to protect our rights.
  • Technical logs of the site: for the period set by the hosting provider.
  • The record of your cookie choice: up to 12 months, after which we will ask for your consent again.
  • Requests from the quiz form (rows in the spreadsheet): no longer than 12 months after the last contact; we will delete them sooner at your request.
  • Statistics data: kept in Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for the period set in the settings of those services; we use it only to analyse site traffic.

7. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data; request rectification; request erasure (“the right to be forgotten”); restrict processing; data portability; object to processing based on legitimate interest; and withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal).

To exercise your rights, write to us at andrei.jasinski@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. This also applies to quiz requests: write to us and we will delete your row from the spreadsheet.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl. If you live in another EU country, you can contact the authority of your country.
