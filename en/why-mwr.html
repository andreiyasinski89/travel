Why start with MWR Life

The trips you already buy can become your own business. No office, no warehouse, no employees.

Start
  1. 01

    You recommend what you use yourself

    There’s nothing to “push”. People buy travel all the time, and you share what you’ve tested on your own trips. Recommending a product you believe in is easier and more honest.

  2. 02

    A start without the investment an ordinary business demands

    Opening a café or a shop means rent, renovation, stock and loans. A franchise means an upfront fee and royalties. Here, getting started costs $588.94 (Turbo), with no rent, no warehouse and no employees.

  3. 03

    No goods, no logistics, no returns

    The product is digital: there is nothing to buy, store, pack or ship. You don’t tie up money in stock.

Freedom
  1. 04

    Work from anywhere

    Everything is done online: a phone and an internet connection. Our team already works in several cities, from Warsaw and Tallinn to Barcelona and Batumi.

  2. 05

    You choose how much time to invest

    You can start alongside your main job, a couple of hours a week, and decide what to do next based on results. Nobody asks you to quit and “jump in at the deep end”.

  3. 06

    Benefits from day one, even before your first partner

    You travel on the club’s terms right away: access to hotel prices below public rates, a bonus points system, cashback, convenient payment terms, reliability, support and a strong community. Even if you never build a team, you and your family still benefit.

Growth
  1. 07

    You’re not alone: training and mentoring

    A new partner gets a step-by-step action plan, a CRM for working with contacts and the support of a mentor. You don’t have to spend years learning from your own mistakes, as you would in a business of your own.

  2. 08

    Ready-made promotion tools

    A website, a quiz for clients, social media content, presentations in several languages. It’s all already done; you just use it and adapt it to yourself.

  3. 09

    Growth depends on your actions, not on a boss

    In a job, your income is limited by a fixed rate and by management’s decisions. Here, the result depends on how many people you introduce to the club and how you support your team. I’ll explain in detail how the rewards work during a video call.

  4. 10

    A circle of people who travel and grow

    It’s a community of people with similar values: freedom, new countries, self-development. Trips together, meetups and sharing experience.

Who this is NOT for

  • people looking for quick money with no effort
  • people who aren’t ready to talk to people and learn
  • people who expect a guaranteed income: no business offers one
